Snake Castles - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

Snake Castles does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal data. The game does not ask you to create an account, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (the castle you have reached) in your device's local storage so you can continue later. This information stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Third parties

The game does not include advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not share data with third parties. The game files are delivered over a secure (HTTPS) connection from GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as part of operating its service. Please see GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding ads or purchases, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact email shown on the Snake Castles page on Google Play.
